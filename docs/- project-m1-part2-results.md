

# Project M1 Part 2 — AI Test Results

## Test 1: General Business Question

**Business:** Sweet Crumbs Bakery

**Question:** Do you make sugar-free cakes?

**Ollama Model:** gemma3:1b

**Prompt:**
You are the assistant of Sweet Crumbs bakery.
Answer the customer in 2 short lines.
Do not invent prices, opening hours, or promises.
If you do not know a business fact, ask staff to confirm it.
Question: Do you make sugar-free cakes?

**Sample AI Answer:**
Please contact Sweet Crumbs bakery staff to confirm
whether sugar-free cakes are available.

**Evaluation:** Useful

**Reason:** The answer avoids claiming that sugar-free cakes are available without confirmation. It gives the customer a practical next step and does not invent a price.

## Overall Conclusion

The Gemma 3.1B model can help prepare short customer-service responses. However, its answers should be checked because an AI model may sometimes provide incorrect or unsupported information.

The chatbot routes questions containing private-information keywords to a local model. This helps keep those questions local, provided the application actually sends them only to the local model.
